# Frozen methodology

This guide summarizes the unchanged protocols; it does not register a new analysis. Exact decisions are in `configs/experiment_protocol_frozen.yaml`, `configs/reconstruction.yaml`, `configs/validation_heasoft_protocol.yaml`, the state protocols and `manuscript/main.tex`.

1. **Labels and identity.** Secure external compact-object labels, canonical physical-source identifiers and reviewed exact aliases are recorded in `data/manifests/`. Uncertain labels/matches are not silently admitted. Grouping uses physical identity, not catalogue strings.
2. **Observation selection.** The primary acquisition selects up to 32 predeclared chronological ranks per verified source. Quality cuts require net rate strictly above 5 counts/s/PCU and at least four usable primary observations per source. Independent processing selects at most eight per source and requires at least two after its quality cuts. Included manifests retain rejected rows; filter `usable` before reporting the primary sample.
3. **Measurements.** Primary inputs reconstruct mission-generated Standard2 source/background/response products. Identity, exposure, channel ordering, detector selection and energy bounds are checked. OGIP source/background scaling produces net rates. Fractional-overlap rebinning gives 43 equal intervals over 5–25 keV and propagates covariance. These are detector-space measurements, not unfolded physical spectra. Independent reduction uses HEASoft 6.37.1 and PCA CALDB 20230501, with PCU2/layer/screening/background/response differences documented in the protocol. No additional manual primary deadtime correction is assumed.
4. **Models and preprocessing.** The main models are a prior baseline, logistic regression and random forest. Standardization is fitted only on training data. Logistic C is selected from 0.01, 0.1, 1, 10; forest depth is selected inside the outer training sources, with 100 trees and minimum leaf size 3. PCA and spectra-plus-errors are sensitivity analyses. No held-out-source observations fit the scaler or tune model parameters.
5. **Evaluation.** Observation-wise folds are a comparator; five source-grouped outer folds and LOSO target unseen systems. The inner holdout is source-disjoint. Training weights limit prolific-source dominance. Source scores use mean log odds with probability clipping at 1e-7; an arithmetic mean probability is also stored but is not the headline aggregation. The decision threshold is fixed at 0.5.
6. **Uncertainty and controls.** Two thousand source-bootstrap resamples produce conditional intervals, not full refitting uncertainty. The primary control uses 50 source-label permutations; independent processing uses 10. Detector matching is common-support restriction, not exact covariate equality. Cohort overlap and residual imbalance are retained in the interpretation.
7. **State gate.** Native evidence and common-regime admissibility are separately assessed. A confirmatory comparison requires five independent sources per class in a justified regime. The final gate is infeasible; proxy diagnostics do not replace that missing experiment.

Tests use explicitly artificial numerical/FITS fixtures for software contracts. Such fixtures never enter scientific results. Legacy neural-network/phenomenological feature scaffolding remains disabled and must not be described as a completed result.
