# Interpretation limits

- The independent BH census is small (7 primary, 9 expanded) and the selected RXTE population is not a population-complete sample.
- Repeated observations do not increase independent-source diversity. Cohort overlap across validations does not create independent replications.
- Detector-space measurements depend on response, detector selection, epoch and processing. Common-support controls leave residual imbalance and unmeasured confounding.
- Published native states are incomplete and not interchangeable between BH and NS taxonomies. Confirmatory physical-state matching is infeasible; state independence is unestablished.
- Conditional source-bootstrap intervals omit refitting and tuning variability. Multiple sensitivities are not independent discoveries.
- Ranking can be useful while threshold classification is unstable. Scores are not established deployment probabilities; no clinical or operational decision threshold is validated.
- Cross-mission classification has not been tested. NuSTAR pilot processing is measurement-development evidence only.
- Public derived-data deposition, author metadata and license choice remain open. Lightweight release checks do not establish full clean-host raw regeneration.
